**Update: We're raising a fund**

## The story

Six months ago, we had an idea to put twelve of the most impressive NYC interns in one apartment in Chelsea. We called our experiment Chelsea Commons. Over the summer, we partnered with 22 VCs and unicorn startups to throw events ranging from hackathons to yacht parties to engage 600+ top university talent.

## The problem

Repeatedly, we've seen that identifying and building relationships with the best young talent is hard for VCs, but not for us; college builders are our peers.

## The plan

Over the course of the next year, we will run ~60 events in six major University cities (NYC, Boston, SF, Atlanta, and Austin). We expect to engage 600 of the most exceptional college students. Out of this group, we will invest in the best teams, supporting the next generation of generational entrepreneurs.

If backing the people we discover first is interesting to you, we'd love to connect. Here's to another experiment.

\- Sachin, Tres, Ethan, Will, Jackson, and the Chelsea Commons team
