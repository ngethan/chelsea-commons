**Update: We're raising a fund**

## 1. Our story

Six months ago, we had an idea to put twelve of the most impressive NYC interns in one apartment in Chelsea. We called our experiment Chelsea Commons. Over the past two months, our intern hacker house expanded to bounds we couldn't initially imagine. Over the summer, we partnered with 12 VCs to throw events ranging from hackathons to yacht parties to engage 600 of the top university talent.

Repeatedly, we've seen that identifying and building relationships with the best young talent is hard. And, our existing friendships with, events for, and proximity in age to young founders allow us to be uniquely good at building these relationships.

Over the course of the next year, we will run ~60 events in six major University cities (NYC, Boston, SF, Atlanta, and Austin) to engage another 600 top young talent through, 2x or 3x our total community count, and invest in the most impressive of this group.

If you're interested in writing follow-on checks on talent that we identify first, please let us know.

\- Sachin, Tres, Ethan, Will, Jackson, and the Chelsea Commons team
