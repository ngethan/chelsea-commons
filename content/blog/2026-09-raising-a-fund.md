To my collaborators,

**Update: We’re raising a fund.**

We’ve seen that identifying and building relationships with the best young talent is hard. Over the past summer, we partnered with 20 VCs to help them with this task.

At the Chelsea Commons, our existing friendships with, events for, and proximity in age to these founders allow us to be uniquely good at building these relationships. Indeed, our 12-person intern hacker house and friend group of New York’s most impressive interns engaged more than 600 of the top college-age talent.

No one knows college age founders better than college-age founders themselves and college age founders are the next generation of generational founders. As operators and facilitators of these circles, we will operate as the scout fund for the next wave.

Over the course of the next year, we will run ~60 events in six major University cities (NYC, Boston, SF, ATL, and Austin) to engage another 600 top young talent through, 2x or 3x our total community count, and invest in the most impressive of this group.

If you’re interested in writing follow-on checks on talent that we identify first, please let us know.

\- Sachin, Tres, Ethan, Will, Jackson, and the Chelsea Commons team
