To my collaborators,

**Update: We're raising a fund.**

This past summer, we built the Chelsea Commons, a 12-person intern hacker house and the heart of 600+ of New York's most impressive interns.

This letter serves as a synopsis of what we accomplished over the summer, plans for the fall, and our vision for the future. Enjoy and we look forward to hearing from you

\- Sachin, Tres, Ethan, Will, Jackson, and the Chelsea Commons team

(P.S. There's no better feeling than people you respect believing in your idea and you are receiving this because you've supported Chelsea Commons :)

## 1. How the summer went

- Hosted 19 events ranging from private dinners to hackathons, sushi-making classes and a yacht party across 9 weeks
- Partnered with 22 VCs and tech companies to host events for 600+ interns from 132 companies
- Within our community, 20+ founders raised money over the summer
- Hosted a hackathon alongside Ramp, OpenAI, and Cursor. [Here is a video we put together!](https://lnkd.in/p/dyDUgXe7)
- Played basketball every Sunday together in Brooklyn followed by a group dinner

## 2. Plans for the Fall

- We've established an exceptional network of students across the country and have especially strong presences in New York, Boston, SF, LA, Atlanta, Nashville, and Austin
- In these cities, we are going to host events for talented students to meet each other and VCs / tech companies

## 3. Our vision for the future

Fundamentally, we believe in 2 things:

- Young technical talent
- New York City's growth in venture and tech

Our goal for next summer: when a top student comes to NYC interested in venture, tech, or startups, we are the first name they think of. The house is fundamental to our thesis, and we are organizing 2 houses (one for girls & guys) starting next year.

## 4. How you can help (thanks for asking!)

- We will be reaching out to each of you regarding interest in us raising a venture fund
- On the hunt for 6+ bedroom townhouses in Manhattan for the summer
- Film/videographers to join our team
- If you know people (or firms) interested in meeting exceptional students, let us know!
